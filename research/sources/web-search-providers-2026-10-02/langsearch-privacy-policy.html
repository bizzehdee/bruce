Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2024-12-19

LangSearch ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal data is handled safely and responsibly. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use LangSearch’s Web Search API ("LangSearch Services").

1. Information We Collect

When you use LangSearch Services, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Information that can identify you, such as your name, email address, and any other contact details you provide.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with LangSearch Services, including search queries, API requests, and usage patterns.
  • Technical Data: Data collected through cookies and similar technologies, such as your IP address, browser type, and device information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and improve LangSearch Services.

  • To monitor and analyze trends, usage patterns, and user behavior to improve our services.

3. Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You may also have the right to object to certain processing activities.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated 2024-12-19.
